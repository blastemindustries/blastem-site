TrackWise Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026 · applies to TrackWise for iOS and Android

TrackWise does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no server to sign in to, and no analytics or advertising in the app. Your budgets, spending, debts and bills are written to storage on your own phone and stay there.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no networking code: it cannot send your information anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to send it and no code to do the sending. The Android build does not even request internet permission. We operate no servers, and we have no way to see what you record in the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter: budgets and categories, payments and expenses, debts, bills, goals, payees and your settings. This is held in the app's own private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionWhat it is for
Camera Scanning a payee's UPI QR code so the id and amount are filled in rather than typed, and reading your partner's screen during an offline sync. Frames are processed on the device as they arrive and are not stored or transmitted.
Notifications Reminders you have asked for: budget thresholds and bills coming due. These are scheduled and delivered on the device; nothing is sent through a push server.
Face ID / fingerprint Locking the app and protecting sensitive actions. The check is performed by the operating system, which only tells the app whether it succeeded. TrackWise never receives or stores biometric data.
Photos (iOS, add only) Saving a payment QR code to your library when you ask for it. The app adds that one image and never reads your library.
Run at startup (Android) Re-scheduling the reminders you have set after the phone restarts.

Payments

TrackWise never moves money and never handles your bank details. When you pay, the app builds a standard UPI link and hands it to the payment app you choose, which takes over from there. The app sees only what you record about the payment afterwards — amount, category, note — and that stays on your phone. We are not a payment service and we are not a party to your transactions.

Receipts

When you share a payment receipt into TrackWise, it is read on the device to pull out the amount and payee. The image is not uploaded and is not kept after the entry is made.

Sharing a budget with a partner

Budgets shared between two phones travel as QR codes shown on one screen and read by the other camera. There is no server and no internet connection in the middle: the two phones must be together, and only the budget records involved are exchanged. Each person keeps their own copy on their own device.

Backups

If you have device backup turned on, your phone's own backup — iCloud on iOS, Google's backup service on Android — may include TrackWise's data along with the rest of your phone. That backup belongs to your Apple or Google account and is governed by their terms, not ours; we cannot see it. You can exclude it in your phone's settings.

Your agreement

When you first open TrackWise it asks you to accept the Terms of Use and this policy, and records on the device that you did, along with which version you accepted. That record stays on your phone; it is not reported to us. If either document changes in a way that affects you, the app asks again rather than assuming your agreement carries over.

Permissions are asked for separately, by your phone, at the moment they are first needed — the camera when you first scan a code, notifications when you first set a reminder. You can withdraw any of them at any time in your phone's settings, and the rest of the app keeps working without them.

Children

TrackWise is a general-purpose budgeting tool and is not directed at children. Since the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your phone, deleting the app removes it. There is no account to close and no copy on our side to request the deletion of. Individual records can be deleted inside the app at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date above. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, that change will be described here and in the store listing before it ships.
