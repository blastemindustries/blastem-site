How to use Zeronet

Zeronet is a location alarm for buses, trains and metros. It wakes you up as you get close to your stop, so you can rest, read or work without watching every station go by.

In three steps: search for your stop, set when you want to be alerted, and tap START TRACKING. Then put your phone away.

When to use it

  • Overnight buses and trains: sleep without worrying about missing your stop at 4 am.
  • Daily commutes: read, listen or nap on the metro or bus and get a nudge before your station.
  • Unfamiliar cities: when you don't know the route or the local names of stops.
  • Anyone travelling who prefers not to keep checking the window, including older relatives you set the trip up for.

Starting a trip

1. Choose your destination

Type a station, bus stop, area or address in Search destination and tap the search button, then pick the right result. You can also long-press the map to drop a pin exactly where you want to get off, and drag the pin to fine-tune it. Places you use often can be saved with the ☆ button and picked later from Saved.

2. Set "Alert at"

This is how far before your stop you get the first, early alert. Good starting points:

JourneyAlert at
City bus or metro1–2 km
Suburban train3–5 km
Intercity bus10–20 km
Overnight train25–50 km (time to wake up and pack)

3. Tap START TRACKING

The screen shows the live distance to your stop, the route line and GPS accuracy. You can lock your phone and put it away: tracking continues in the background, with a notification on Android and a Live Activity on iPhone's lock screen.

What happens on the way

  • Early alert at your "Alert at" distance: an alarm, vibration and a spoken message such as "2 kilometres to Secunderabad Station". Tap Stop, or Remind in 5 km to be alerted again closer.
  • Arrival alarm when you reach your destination: a full-screen alarm with a reminder to check your belongings. Tap Stop alarm to end the trip.
  • To end a trip early, tap CANCEL TRACKING.

Making alerts reliable

On Android

  • Allow Location and Notifications when asked.
  • Allow full-screen alerts (Settings → Reliability) so the alarm appears over the lock screen.
  • Set Battery optimisation to "Don't optimise" for Zeronet (Settings → Reliability). This matters most on Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus and Samsung phones, which close background apps aggressively.
  • Don't swipe Zeronet away from recent apps during a trip.

On iPhone

  • Allow location Always when asked during your first trip. iPhone can then wake Zeronet at your destination even if the app was closed.
  • Allow Alarms when asked, so the alarm rings full screen even on silent or in a Focus mode.
  • Allow Live Activities to see your trip on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

On both

  • Keep your phone charged, especially on long journeys.
  • Check that the alarm volume isn't at zero. Use Settings → Test alarm before your first trip.
  • If you use headphones, the alarm may play through them.

Settings

  • Alarm tone and Alert style: sound and vibration, sound only, or vibration only.
  • Gradually increase volume: starts quietly and gets louder, so you wake gently.
  • Spoken alerts and Voice language: English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, मराठी, বাংলা or ગુજરાતી. Use Test voice to hear it.
  • Arrival radius: how close counts as "arrived" (500 m is recommended; larger stations may need more).

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once your trip has started. Distance and alarms use GPS, which doesn't need mobile data. You need internet to search for a place and to load new map areas; saved places work offline.

Does it drain my battery?

GPS uses some battery while a trip is active, and Zeronet stops tracking as soon as you arrive or cancel. On long journeys, start with a good charge or keep the phone plugged in.

Why was my alert late?

Usually because of weak GPS (tunnels, underground stations, inside metal train coaches), battery saving stopping the app, or approximate location being allowed instead of precise. See "Making alerts reliable" above. The distance shown is a straight line, so on winding routes you may want a larger "Alert at".

Is my location shared?

No. Your location, trips and saved places stay on your phone, and there's no account. Searches go to OpenStreetMap (Android) or Apple Maps (iPhone). See the Privacy Policy.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Zeronet free. They appear only on the main and history screens, never on the alarm screen or while an alarm is ringing.

Can I use it while driving?

Please don't interact with the app while driving. Set up your trip before you start, or ask a passenger.

Zeronet is a travel aid, not a guarantee. Stay aware of your stop, especially on your first trips.
