    How webuild.

    Four rules every Blastem app has to pass before it ships.

    01
    RULE 01

    Local first

    Your data lives on your phone. If a feature can work without a server, it does. Deleting the app deletes everything, because there is no copy anywhere else.

    ↳ TrackWise collects no personal data
    RULE 02

    Built for the moment

    We design for the exact situation you are in: half-asleep in the last row of a train, or standing at a checkout. Big targets, loud alarms, nothing to read.

    ↳ Zeronet: four taps at the start, none after
    RULE 03

    Honest permissions

    Every permission is asked for at the moment it is first needed, explained in plain words, and can be withdrawn without breaking the rest of the app.

    ↳ Camera only when you first scan a code
    RULE 04

    Nothing in the middle

    No accounts to make, no servers between two phones, no one reading your receipts. When two people share a budget, it travels by QR code, screen to camera.

    ↳ 0 servers involved in budget sharing
