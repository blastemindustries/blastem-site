How a trip runs

Four taps at the start, nothing to watch after that.

The whole point is that you stop checking the map. Set it once when you board.

Pick the destination

Search for a place, or long-press the map to drop a pin and drag it to the exact spot.

Set the alert distance

Choose how far out the alarm should fire. Saved places remember their own distance.

Start tracking

Lock the phone. A notification and a home-screen widget keep the distance and ETA in view.

Get woken

The alarm sounds at your alert distance, and again when you reach the arrival radius.

What it does

Built for the person asleep in the last row.

Alarm

Loud enough, on the lock screen

Full-screen alarm over the lock screen with Stop and Snooze. Pick your own tone, or let the volume climb from quiet to full over 10 seconds to a minute so it wakes you gently.

Silent mode

Vibrate only, for a quiet coach

Sound and vibration, sound alone, or vibration alone. Zeronet warns you if your alarm volume is at zero, before the trip rather than after your stop.

Voice

Spoken alerts in your language

The distance and destination are announced before the alarm, in the language you choose.

“Proximity alert. 5 kilometres to Kacheguda.”

Snooze

Remind me in 5 km

Not ready to get up? One tap sends the alarm away and brings it back five kilometres later, with the voice confirming when it will return.

Saved

Home and Office, one tap away

Save the stops you use every day with a name and their own alert distance, then start a trip without touching the map.

History

Every trip, and how it ended

Arrived, cancelled or interrupted, with distance travelled and the time it took. Tap any trip to set the same destination again.

Widget

Distance left on the home screen

The active trip, how far is left and the ETA, without opening the app.

Arrival

“You have arrived. Check your belongings.”

A second alarm fires inside the arrival radius you set, so you get off with everything you got on with.

Voice alerts

Nine languages, each in its own script.

Choose the voice language in settings and test it before you travel.

What it needs

Straight about the phone it runs on.

Platform
Android phones and tablets with GPS.
Location
Precise location while a trip is running. Approximate location makes alerts late.
Maps
OpenStreetMap. Searching for a place uses the internet; tracking to it does not.
Battery
Set Zeronet to “Don’t optimise” so long trips are never cut short.
