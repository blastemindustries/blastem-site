1. What Zeronet does

Zeronet is a travel aid. You pick a destination, and the app uses your phone's location to show the remaining distance and to sound an alert when you are within a distance you choose, and again when you arrive.

2. Important safety notice

Zeronet is an aid only. Do not rely on it as your only way of knowing when to get off or stop.

Alerts can be late, early or missed. This can happen for reasons outside our control, including:

  • weak, inaccurate or unavailable GPS or mobile signal (for example in tunnels, underground, inside vehicles or between tall buildings);
  • approximate rather than precise location being allowed;
  • the phone's operating system, battery saver or manufacturer settings stopping or pausing the app;
  • a low or flat battery, the phone being switched off, or the app being closed or force-stopped;
  • sound being muted, alarm volume set to zero, Do Not Disturb or Focus modes, or headphones being connected;
  • permissions such as location, notifications, full-screen alerts or alarms being denied or turned off;
  • wrong map, search or address data from third-party providers;
  • the straight-line distance the app measures differing from the distance along roads or rails, so time estimates are approximate.

You remain responsible for your own journey, including watching for your stop, following announcements and instructions from transport staff, and keeping your belongings safe. Keep your phone charged and check that alerts work (Settings → Test alarm) before relying on them.

Do not interact with the app while driving or operating any vehicle or machinery. Set up your trip before you start moving, or have a passenger do it. Obey all traffic laws.

Zeronet is not a navigation, emergency, medical or safety-of-life service and must not be used where a missed or wrong alert could cause injury, death, loss or damage.
