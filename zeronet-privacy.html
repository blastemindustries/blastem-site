Zeronet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Applies to the Zeronet apps for Android and iOS

In short: Zeronet uses your location on your phone to tell you how far you are from your destination and to wake you before you arrive. We do not have accounts, servers or analytics, and we never receive your location. Your trips, saved places and settings stay on your device. Zeronet is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. On Android this uses your device's advertising ID; on iPhone, Apple's advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when asked. Some information is sent to the map, search, location, voice and advertising services the app relies on, as described below.

1. Who we are

Zeronet is published by Blastem Industries ("we", "us"). Contact: admin@blastemindustries.com.

2. Information the app uses

Location

Zeronet uses your device's precise or approximate location (whichever you allow) to:

  • show where you are on the map and measure the distance to the destination you pick;
  • during a trip you start, keep measuring that distance while the app is in the background or the phone is locked, so it can alert you near your destination;
  • on iOS, if you allow "Always" location, ask iOS to watch the area around your destination so the arrival alarm can still ring if the app was closed.

Location is only tracked in the background while a trip is active. Tracking stops when you arrive, stop the alarm or cancel the trip. On Android, a notification is shown for as long as tracking runs. Your location is processed on your device and is not sent to us.

Destinations, trip history and saved places

The places you search for or pin, your trip history (destination, start and end time, distance travelled, outcome) and saved destinations (name, coordinates and alert distance) are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

Settings

Your choices (alarm tone, alert style, voice language, arrival radius and similar) are stored on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or payment details. There is no sign-in and no analytics software. We do not sell or rent personal data. Both apps show ads (see section 4).

4. Advertising

Zeronet is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob on the main screen and the trip history screen. Ads are never shown on the alarm screen or while an alarm is ringing, and there are no full-screen ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG or lower).

Google acts as an independent controller of the data its ad services collect. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads). Ads may still appear, but they will be less relevant to you.

On iPhone, Zeronet asks for permission to track using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Apple's advertising identifier is not shared with Google and ads are less personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Permissions

  • Location (Android "while in use", iOS "While Using" or "Always"): required to measure the distance to your destination. You can change this at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Notifications: to show the trip status and the arrival alert.
  • Full-screen alerts (Android) / Alarms (iOS): so the arrival alarm can appear over the lock screen and ring when the phone is on silent.
  • Vibration and keep-awake (Android): to vibrate and keep the alarm playing.

6. Storage, backups and retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it: clear trip history, delete saved places in the app, or uninstall the app. Your phone's own backup service (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backup on iOS) may include the app's data in your device backup, under your account with that provider. We cannot access those backups.

7. Your choices and rights

You control all data in the app: you can view and delete trip history and saved places at any time, withdraw permissions in your phone settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Because we do not receive or hold your personal data, we usually cannot retrieve, correct or delete it for you; you can do this directly on your device. For ad choices, see section 4. If you have a question or complaint, contact us using the details below. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

8. Children

Zeronet is not directed at children under 13, and its ads are not treated as directed at children. Where local law treats users under 18 as children (as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 does), the app should be used with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

9. Security

Information stored by the app is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your phone's operating system. Connections to the search and map services use encrypted HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your phone locked and up to date.

10. International use

The third-party services listed above may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, the European Union and the United Kingdom, under their own safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy, change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or in the app store release notes.
